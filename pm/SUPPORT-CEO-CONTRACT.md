# Support state after CEO review

Bastion's temporary gift is one independent `sharedWard` token, with owner and eight-second expiry. `h.ward`, `wardMax`, own initialization and recharge belong solely to the recipient's weapon. A valid gift absorbs first, credits its donor's actual prevented damage, and produces neither retaliation nor another gift. Own initialization, capacity changes, recharge and overclock do not consume this token. Expiration is checked in hurtPlayer before any damage path: swept hostile contacts pass their actual entry time; callers without a contact time, including legacy early AI hazards, use room time. The optional fourth argument `hurtPlayer(h,damage,source,contactAt)` affects only temporary gift eligibility, never the room clock, own recharge or other perks. A future integration with dash body contacts must pass the actual body contact time through the same argument; this branch does not import dash mechanics. Snapshot row13 remains the own charge count; sparse top-level wardGift=[pid,until] carries temporary tokens. Dome attack radius and its visual read own charges; shield glyphs display own+valid gift. Key/delta recovery preserves both states; the player row stays15 fields and unreleased PROTO30 remains distinct from combat-only main29.

Gift windows are `[grantedAt, until)`, with `until = actualContactAt + 8`. The same actual contact time passes through `hurtPlayer`, gift consumption, `wardAbsorb`, recipient eligibility and the grant. At exact expiry a new donation is eligible; a receiver at exact expiry cannot consume the old one. Radius, recipient liveness, offline state and own capacity are checked before touching another hero's token, so an out-of-range donor cannot expire or replace it. Own charge initialization, recharge, retaliation, other perks and effect clocks still use room time.

Legacy early AI runs before the chronological projectile phase and can grant at the frame endpoint. Such a grant is a private conditional reservation, not a charge available before its birth. AI grant eligibility projects gift windows without deleting an older token still live at an earlier contact. Earlier projectile donations and consumption run first in gift time: an earlier token that survives until the reservation's birth rejects it; a spent or expired earlier token permits it. An actual early-AI hit keeps its existing phase priority; no AI, damage, collisions or RNG are replayed. Endpoint settlement also visits downed/offline recipients so a rejected reservation cannot activate after revival. Snapshots project the active window without mutating reservations.

There is one usable gifted charge per recipient. In the native frame, early AI grants share the endpoint timestamp; subsequent grants see the first reservation and cannot append another for that recipient. At most one pending grant per recipient (eight per room) can survive into the projectile phase, plus at most one earlier active token. Each hold is activated or rejected by endpoint settlement in the same frame, without a historical ledger. The private birth, range and reservation fields are never serialized; `wardGift=[pid,until]`, player row15 and PROTO30 are unchanged. Dash body-contact integration must forward actual body time to `hurtPlayer` and preserve the same gift window contract.


Cold slow and non-cold Drag maintain independent active owner/strength/expiry entries. An owner's equal-strength applications may coalesce to their maximum expiry; different strengths retain independent deadlines. Projection chooses the strongest active source, then the longest-lived equal-strength source. Expired stronger effects reveal only still-active weaker effects. Boss cold-slow cap remains 40%; ordinary cold-slow cap remains 70%. Fire consuming cold control also clears its active sources. Drag from affinity and Pitch has an owner and participates in the same unique enemy-seconds union as freeze, root and cold slow. Credit is emitted by an AI branch that actually uses control (paused action or shorter movement), or by the native body-contact loop when freeze suppresses an otherwise eligible contact. Body credit is evaluated after early projectiles establish real protection, without replaying AI or collisions. Fixed-speed charges, aim/rest/wind waits and contact-limited movement do not earn time for ignored slow statuses. Root at contact earns nothing unless it prevents an available action; freeze can still suppress actual body contact. Shield turning is an available defensive action even when locomotion is contact-limited. Each projected endpoint has its own read-only mathematical separation in native grid candidate order and neighbor limit, then the guard leash clamp; the actual slowed separation displacement is never reused. Coincident random-overlap frames are unresolved and conservatively not credited, rather than called zero-effect. No extra RNG, actor/grid mutation or actual collision/AI/query call is made by the observer. Ordinary movement and controlled gunner movement still count. A Room-owned Set bounds credit to one owner/interval for each enemy across AI and all allied body checks. The Set is cleared at each new active time/direct AI interval, reset and finish; it cannot retain past-run actors. Hero protection does not erase an independently prevented shield turn or cast. Future dash-contact integration must use the same union budget and actual protection at the body event, rather than importing the endpoint assumption.

Ally damage boost requires an actual different hero attacker; an enemy/environment hit with no hero cannot contribute to allyDamage. Healing field limits and other mark budgets remain unchanged.

The total mark bonus (supportDamage) includes the caster own attacks, other heroes and hostile/environment explosions. It is not limited to attacking players. The allyDamage subset still requires a real different hero attacker. Results and saved-history cards state this distinction explicitly.
