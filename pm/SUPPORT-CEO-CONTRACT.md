# Support state after CEO review

Bastion's temporary gift is one independent `sharedWard` token, with owner and eight-second expiry. `h.ward`, `wardMax`, own initialization and recharge belong solely to the recipient's weapon. A valid gift absorbs first, credits its donor's actual prevented damage, and produces neither retaliation nor another gift. Own initialization, capacity changes, recharge and overclock do not consume this token. Expiration is checked in hurtPlayer before any damage path, including AI hazards that precede updatePlayers. Snapshot row13 remains the own charge count; sparse top-level wardGift=[pid,until] carries temporary tokens. Dome attack radius and its visual read own charges; shield glyphs display own+valid gift. Key/delta recovery preserves both states; the player row stays15 fields and unreleased PROTO29 remains distinct from main28.

Cold slow and non-cold Drag maintain independent active owner/strength/expiry entries. An owner's equal-strength applications may coalesce to their maximum expiry; different strengths retain independent deadlines. Projection chooses the strongest active source, then the longest-lived equal-strength source. Expired stronger effects reveal only still-active weaker effects. Boss cold-slow cap remains 40%; ordinary cold-slow cap remains 70%. Fire consuming cold control also clears its active sources. Drag from affinity and Pitch has an owner and participates in the same unique enemy-seconds union as freeze, root and cold slow. Overlap credits one owner once, with freeze/root/cold/Drag priority, rather than stacking contribution counters.

Ally damage boost requires an actual different hero attacker; an enemy/environment hit with no hero cannot contribute to allyDamage. Healing field limits and other mark budgets remain unchanged.

The total mark bonus (supportDamage) includes the caster own attacks, other heroes and hostile/environment explosions. It is not limited to attacking players. The allyDamage subset still requires a real different hero attacker. Results and saved-history cards state this distinction explicitly.
